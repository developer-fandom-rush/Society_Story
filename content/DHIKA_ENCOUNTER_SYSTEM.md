# Dhika Encounter Atlas — Non-Graphic Action Taxonomy & Character Checklist

> This is a structured, non-graphic planning guide. Action labels are possibilities, not evidence that they occurred in original canon.

## Source and classification rules

- Original source ends at author EP41; original author episode numbers are separate from the reconciled secondary ledger.
- Every action has its own evidence state: actual, direct sight, accidental sight, imagined, dreamed, recalled, reported, inferred, declined, off-page, and consent-based witness.
- A source-verified relationship does not imply every possible action is source verified. Unknown remains unknown.
- No penetration: manual external touch, external friction and some external oral contact can be non-penetrative. Oral sex generally may involve penetration, so label cautiously rather than claiming it is always non-penetrative.
- Observation by John, solo masturbation and imagined self-stimulation are distinct actions.
- A character's 'no' or stop immediately ends that act; do not use source boundary violations as romantic templates.

## Slow-burn rhythm

1. Practical reason / social setup
2. Attraction, proximity or internal curiosity
3. One clearly scoped encounter or relationship beat
4. Explicit stop, interruption, withdrawal or intentional end
5. Private emotional aftermath and knowledge tracking
6. Ordinary life / alternate subplot / days apart
7. New meeting only after its own setup and renewed consent

### Hard rules

- Do not force gym, swimming, tuition, Leo return, Stranger, Uncle and couples' games into the same episode.
- A high-intensity encounter gets at least one meaningful post-encounter recovery scene and normally an intervening episode/other storyline before the same pairing escalates again.
- One substantial romantic/sexual escalation per relationship per episode maximum; zero-escalation episodes are welcome.
- Actual, imagined, recalled, reported, sighted, accidentally seen and specifically witnessed are distinct statuses in the ledger.
- Self-masturbation is a solo action; a character imagining it is not a completed solo act; masturbation witnessed by John needs explicit participant/viewer consent.
- John may know, hear, watch, not watch or be misinformed: each is a different state. Viewer participation must not be presumed.
- An interrupted encounter doesn't necessarily imply consensual continuation later; explicit no and stop requests end contact.
- Do not infer nudity consent from clothing, fitness, lesson, medical or work interactions.
- Prevent impossible simultaneous locations. Track gym, tuition, swimming, work, childcare, repair and private scenes on one timeline.
- Prior action milestones never reset to 'first' without reliable verification from original source.
- Women–women romance, married-couple games, Nisha–Uncle and Leo–Nisha remain separate proposed lanes with no automatic physical crossover.
- All sexual action names remain non-graphic/off-page; original source remains separate from future speculation.
