> Society Wives — Episode 1, Prologue. One building, five doors, one long Gurgaon weekday where nothing happens… almost.

Gurgaon mein har society ek jaisi lagti hai bahar se. Glass ka lobby, guard ki register, "Visitors please park outside" ka board, aur lift ke mirror mein woh halki si dhoop jo subah saat baje andar tak aa jaati hai. Hamari society bhi aisi hi thi — Tower C, ninth floor tak ke flats, har door pe brass ki nameplate. Mr. & Mrs. Malhotra. Kapoor Residence. Dr. Nikhil & Dr. Meenakshi. Respectable naam. Respectable doors. Aur un doors ke peeche woh cheezein jo nameplate pe kabhi nahi likhi jaati.

Subah ki society ki apni awaaz hoti hai. Basement parking mein car ka beep, lift ka ding, milkman ki cycle ki ghanti, aur corridor ke end pe khadi aunties ki aankhein — jo sab dekhti hain, sab yaad rakhti hain, aur shaam ki walk pe sab ek doosre ko bata deti hain. Is building mein kuch bhi chhupta nahi tha. Bas… kuch cheezein dikhti nahi thi.

Paanch auratein thi is kahani ki. Paanch biwiyan, paanch doors, ek hi building ki hawa.

Ankita sabse pehle dikhti thi, kyunki uski saree kabhi galat nahi hoti thi. Pastel pink, pleats ekdum seedhi, pallu pe ek chhoti si safety pin jo usne college ke pehle saal se lagana shuru kiya tha. Physics padhati thi — Assistant Professor, private college, Cyber City wali road. Students usko "Ma'am" bolte the, aur jab woh corridor se guzarti thi toh unki awaaz thodi dheemi ho jaati thi. Ankita ko pata tha. Woh hamesha ignore karti thi. Hamesha.

Galleria ki boutique uski thi, bridal orders uske the, aur uske blouses — woh khud stitch karwati thi, back pe hooks, deep cut, "designer ka apna sample, yaar." Jab woh kapda feel karti thi toh ungliyan aise chalti thi jaise kapda nahi, kisi ki skin ho.

Aur Meenakshi. Lilac scrubs, white coat haath pe, Sohna Road ka clinic. Doctor thi, toh body ke baare mein sabse kam sharmati thi — aur shayad isliye sabse zyada samajhti thi ki uski body ko log kaise dekhte hain. Uski hasi soft thi. Uski thakaan bhi soft thi. Kabir apne product launch mein itna doobha ki ghar ek charger point ban gaya tha. Arjun ka gym subah 7:40 pe shuru hota tha aur uski duniya wahin khatam. Vikram ghar pe hi tha, work-from-home, lekin uske calls ka door hamesha band rehta. Aur Nikhil — Nikhil flights mein rehta tha, airport cabs mein, conference hotels mein. Paanch pati, paanch shapes jo ghar mein dikhte the, lekin ghar ko dekhte nahi the.

Aur phir corridor ke woh log the. Ek retired army wala jo har subah landing pe "beta" bolta tha. Ek society treasurer jo water meter ke bahane har mahine door pe aata tha. Ek purane family friend jinke paas ek ghar ki spare chaabi thi. Ek uncle jo bank ka kaam "jaldi" karwane ke dawe karta tha. Ek aur jo har baat mein Nikhil ka naam le leta tha. Yeh kahani unke baare mein nahi hai, waise. Yeh kahani un paanch auraton ke andar ki hai — jahan ek awaaz kehti hai, "Tu achhi biwi hai, ignore kar," aur doosri, dheemi, thodi garam awaaz kehti hai, "Tujhe pata hai woh dekh rahe hain. Aur tujhe achha lagta hai." Angel aur Devil. Dono sach bolte hain. Dono jeetna chahte hain.

Yeh Episode 1 hai. Woh din jab kuch hota nahi. Koi line cross nahi hoti. Bas… almost. Ek chaabi gayab hoti hai. Ek drawing badal jaati hai. Ek saree swap hoti hai. Ek signature match nahi karta. Corridor mein kisi ne dheere se apna darwaza khola. Dekha. Smile kiya. Aur wapas band kar diya.

Din shuru ho chuka tha.
